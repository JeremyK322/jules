Lunar Constraint Map Guide

3D Globe Navigation

Interact with the 3D lunar globe using mouse, trackpad, or touchscreen controls:

  • Mouse Controls:
    • Rotate / Orbit: Click and drag with the primary (left) mouse button to rotate around the Moon.
    • Zoom: Scroll the mouse wheel up to zoom in, and down to zoom out.
  • Touchscreen & Trackpad Controls:
    • Rotate / Orbit: Touch and drag with one finger, or click-and-drag on a trackpad to orbit the lunar globe.
    • Pinch to Zoom: Pinch two fingers together or spread them apart on touchscreen devices or touchpads to adjust zoom level.

What is the Lunar Constraint Map (LCM)?

The Lunar Constraint Map is an interactive 3D geospatial tool designed to visualize spatial constraints, physical landforms, exploration heritage sites, resource zones, and regulatory status across the Moon's surface.

Outer Space Treaty (OST) Legal Framework

LCM incorporates key governance principles established under international space law:

Article 6 (Art. 6) – State Responsibility:

States Parties bear international responsibility for national activities in outer space, whether carried out by governmental agencies or non-governmental entities (private sector). Private missions require authorization and continuing supervision by the appropriate State.

Article 9 (Art. 9) – Due Regard & Harmful Interference:

Activities in outer space must be conducted with due regard to the corresponding interests of all other States. If a state or entity has reason to believe an activity would cause potentially harmful interference with lunar activities of others, international consultation is required prior to proceeding.

Article 11 (Art. 11) – Information Sharing:

States agree to inform the UN Secretary-General, the public, and the international scientific community to the greatest extent feasible of the nature, conduct, locations, and results of lunar activities.

Settings Control Panel

Click the Settings button at the top-right corner to open configuration controls:

  • Load GLB: Load a custom 3D mesh model (`.glb` or `.gltf` file) for the lunar sphere. You can also drag and drop model files anywhere onto the canvas.
  • Reset View: Resets camera position and target orientation to default coordinates.
  • Art. VI State Mode: Switches feature disc/line rendering to OST Article VI State Responsibility colors and toggles the bottom-right State Parties Legend panel.
  • Info Debug Panel: Shows or hides the real-time telemetry/camera debug panel at the bottom-right corner of the view.
  • Lock N/S Pole: Restricts camera orbit control to the equatorial plane (locking vertical North/South polar tilt).
  • Auto-rotate: Toggles continuous slow rotation of the lunar globe.
  • Glow Opacity: Adjusts the transparency slider (0.00 to 1.00) for active 3D surface feature discs and highlight overlays.

Moon Layers Panel

The Moon Layers panel at the top-left controls active features and spatial overlays:

  • Global Checkbox (top): Master toggle to enable or disable all categories and layers simultaneously.
  • Category Accordion: Click any category header (e.g., Physical Features, Extractable, Spatial) to expand or collapse its layer list.
  • Discs Checkbox: Toggle visibility of 3D surface discs for all features within that category without unchecking individual layers.
  • Layer Items & Feature Sub-lists: Click the arrow beside a layer item to expand individual point/orbit features. You can toggle specific landing sites, craters, or satellites individually.

OST Article VI State Responsibility Mode

Art. VI State Mode visualizes state party authorization and supervision responsibilities under the Outer Space Treaty:

  • State Party Color Palette: Toggling State Mode recolors surface landing sites, bases, and orbital tracks according to responsible State Parties (USA, USSR Historical, Russian Federation, China, India, Japan, South Korea, ESA Member States, Israel, Pakistan).
  • State Parties Legend Panel: Appears in the bottom-right corner when State Mode is ON, listing active State Parties and international intergovernmental mechanisms (e.g., ESA joint responsibility under Art. VI & XIII).
  • Dual Indicator Dots: Feature sub-lists in the Moon Layers sidebar display both resource category dots and state responsibility dots.
  • Non-Appropriation Principle (Art. II): Natural physical landforms (craters, maria, mountain ranges) retain `state: null` designations to reflect OST Article II non-appropriation.

On-Screen Information & Debug Panels

Bottom-Right Info Debug Panel

Disabled by default, but can be enabled via the Info Debug Panel toggle in Settings:

  • Dist: Camera distance multiplier relative to the center of the Moon.
  • Lat / Lon: Latitude (deg N/S) and Longitude (deg E/W) of current camera viewpoint.
  • Sens: Dynamic orbital rotation sensitivity based on zoom level.
  • Rot: Auto-rotation state (`ON` / `OFF`).
  • Collapse Button `[-] / [+]`: Minimizes the debug panel to a compact header bar.

Bottom-Left Panel (Centre Overlap Status)

  • Loc: Coordinates on the lunar surface directly at the center of your screen view.
  • Status: Shows active zone constraints, applicable OST Articles (Art. 6 / Art. 9 / Art. 2), tier levels, state responsibility tags, and feature names located at the center crosshairs, or displays "Open" if no active constraints overlap.
